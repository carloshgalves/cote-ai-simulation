# Pilot V9 — autonomous ordinary life

Delivery 2/9. New journals use V9; earlier saves retain the original behavior and codec. All changes are scoped to the local Go pilot.

- Routine policy uses the actor's own admission dispositions, current goals, body, inventory, legal offers and accepted commitments. It is independent of resolution tier. Sparse profiles use a named deterministic seeded fallback, not a canonical assertion.
- Ordinary study, lessons, exercise, reading, relaxation, meals, sleep and discretionary leisure use engine-owned actions. A seeded optional paid leisure action is limited to one per actor/day and requires a balance of at least 10,000 PP. No purchases or social effects are narrated as successful before resolution.
- Shared completed courtyard leisure creates a bilateral encounter without scripted dialogue or automatic trust. Only participants see the event; solo leisure does not fabricate another participant.
- Endogenous opportunities use staggered free-period windows and one-, two- or three-day cadences based on own goals/dispositions and plan presence. A first-day introduction window is available to high-initiative actors. Addressed stimuli and deliberate rechecks retain priority; no fixed quota suppresses important events.
- Existing accepted appointments may cause ordinary travel; needs are handled by local actions. A routine task segment is bounded by the next public period or upcoming departure where possible; meals and paid leisure are indivisible.
- These are provisional behavioral priors, not calibrated canon preferences, full club membership, or a replacement for the formal Embodiment subdomain. A dedicated validation delivery must assess actual trajectory quality, social coverage, cost, and fidelity before claiming those outcomes.
