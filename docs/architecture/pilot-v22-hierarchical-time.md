# V22 hierarchical time

The new journal version uses a persistent deterministic policy independent of UI playback mode. Ordinary discretionary interests use the local routine prior; source-grounded strategic goal terms and plans route to a model. Discretionary strategic opportunities without plans are bounded at one per day; addressed stimuli, active exam decisions and explicit timed deliberation retain priority. This conservative lexical classifier is provisional, not a learned behavior model. Free morning uses the same personal leisure prior rather than compulsory study. Local interactions record actual overlap of socializing activities (>=10 minutes, once per pair/day, private audience), never invented dialogue, trust, belief or alliance.

Repeatable lessons, study, staff work and rest span a school period or earlier public/deadline/physiological boundary. Leisure remains at most hourly when extended; meals, moves, exercise, purchases and exam submissions are not aggregated. Coarse activities can be interrupted by addressed stimulus; actual elapsed body effects persist and the partial activity is recorded. Relax/socialize spans remain contactable for detailed dialogue. Plans and commitments prevent aggregation. Old activity fields are omitted for pre-V22 journals. No claim of equivalence of V21 and V22 trajectories; exact V22 replay, including batch versus individual execution, is required.

Routine macros commit up to 96 normal rounds, stopping before required inference; meaningful events are resolved in the normal reducer and retain all audit records. Routine validation avoids reconstructing full historical cognition for a provenance-checked deterministic choice; detailed steps compute the canonical transition once. UI shows target, committed elapsed fraction, mode, simulation provider attempts (including retries, not diagnostic route tests) and committed rounds. Counts are process-local. Existing router intentionally serializes provider calls for quota/format negotiation; not changed. No promise of a wall-clock acceleration factor or full-year viability with the existing 32 MiB journal. Focused tests authored for period boundaries, stimulus interruption, strategic routing, co-presence, and exact macro replay. Go execution, independent review and actual cost/fidelity benchmarking pending.

Standalone strategic goals also produce an opportunity even when they have no ordinary social/academic keywords. The advance summary freezes its final counters so later runs do not change the displayed result.
