# V25 social cognition

Directional, bounded subjective assessments of trust, affinity, rivalry, esteem (localized reputation) and nonreciprocal affective interest. Each model proposal needs an accessible relevant experienced event, one use per dimension/evidence, and validated peer. Affective interest is restricted to same-grade students with at least three dialogue interactions. Neither co-presence nor a single assessment creates a relationship status, romance or universal reputation. Existing objective encounter and commitment counters remain. Ordinary sessions cap at 8 turns/20 minutes; active plan or exam sessions allow 24/60; lexical stale fence remains, and voluntary closures are logged. Plan deadlines use actual versioned horizon. This does not implement full semantic novelty or calibrated social psychology; tests and real trajectory evaluation pending.
