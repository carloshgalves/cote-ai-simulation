# Operator-authorized source editions

Decision: 2026-10-22. The operator may designate supplied translations (including unofficial editions) as accepted **project authority** for COTE Simulation canon packs. This changes the project's internal evidence policy, not the publishing status, authorship or licensing of an edition.

## Separate gates

1. **SOURCE_ACCEPTED_BY_OPERATOR**: source identified by translator/version, digest if available, scope and approval. Its pages may support extraction, audit and eventual approved records. No Japanese/original/official translation is mandatory for this operator-approved corpus.
2. **RECORD_AUDITED**: claim or behavioral evidence includes exact locator, story/knowledge time, narrative mode, source of claims, epistemic status and conflicts. Approval of a whole edition never silently changes unreviewed individual claims to VERIFIED.
3. **COMPILED_CANON**: only records that satisfy record-level schema, actor disclosure, temporal/divergence and conflict gates may enter engine-usable derived knowledge. Existing automatic gates must NOT be bypassed by setting VERIFIED en masse.

Retain bibliographic distinction: japanese original and licensed editions remain clearly identified, fan translations are labeled fan translations; all can be separately admitted by operator policy. Conflicting readings remain disputes with a preferred project edition rather than being quietly resolved.

Never publish full copyrighted source text or its images in the public repo. Store original uploads only in private ignored storage. Public packs store original analytical paraphrases and references.
